# 12. Glossary

> Exercise: **E10** (and add terms in any session). English exam term first, German in brackets.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Building block (Baustein) | Any structural element of the architecture; a component is a special form of one. |
| Constraint (Randbedingung) | A restriction on the design freedom that isn't negotiable, at least for now. |
| Cross-cutting concept (Querschnittskonzept) | A solution approach that applies across several building blocks. |
| Quality scenario (Qualitätsszenario) | Context, stimulus, response and measurement describing a required quality. |
| Stakeholder (Stakeholder) | A person or role with an interest in the system or its architecture. |
| IMU | Inertial measurement unit (accelerometer + gyroscope); here the MPU-6050. |
